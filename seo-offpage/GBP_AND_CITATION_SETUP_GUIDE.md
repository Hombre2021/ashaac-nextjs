## 2. Review Velocity System (Get 2–3 New Reviews Every Week)

Google's algorithm prioritizes **Review Recency & Velocity** over raw lifetime review count. A dormant profile with 25 reviews gets outranked by an active competitor getting fresh reviews each week.

### Step 1: Grab Your Short Review Link
1. Go to your GBP dashboard.
2. Click **"Ask for reviews"** (or **"Get more reviews"**).
3. Copy your unique short link (format: `https://g.page/r/[CODE]/review`).

### Step 2: Customer Review Request SMS Templates

#### Template 1: Same-Day Post-Service SMS (Send within 2 hours of job completion)
> Hi [Customer Name], thank you for choosing All Solutions Heating and Air today! If you have 30 seconds, would you mind sharing your experience in a quick Google review? It helps our local West Jordan family business tremendously: [PASTE_GBP_REVIEW_LINK]

#### Template 2: Equipment Install / Major Project Follow-Up (Send 48 hours later)
> Hi [Customer Name], this is Leandro from All Solutions HVAC checking in. We wanted to make sure your new [furnace / AC / heat pump] is running perfectly. If you are happy with the work, we would love a quick review here: [PASTE_GBP_REVIEW_LINK]. Thank you!

#### Template 3: Friendly 7-Day Gentle Reminder
> Hi [Customer Name], quick note from All Solutions HVAC. If you have a moment to rate our service, here is the direct link: [PASTE_GBP_REVIEW_LINK]. We really appreciate your support!

### Step 3: Keyword-Rich Review Responses
Always reply within 24–48 hours. Google indexes the text inside review responses:
> *"Thank you, [Name]! We're glad we could get your AC cooling again quickly in [City]. If you ever need furnace repair or seasonal tune-ups, call or text us at 801-755-3040."*
